About me

Hi, I'm Quinn Lambert.

Data Science and Business student at Northeastern, building my own research tools on the side.

I'm a sophomore at Northeastern University studying Data Science and Business. I started out on the Oakland campus in 2025 and moved to Boston this fall, which honestly is a pretty small move compared to how I grew up. My family relocated about every two years for my parent's work, so I spent chunks of my childhood in places like Kosovo and Switzerland before ending up in Vermont. Summers, I work at a snack bar in South Hero, running the grill and the fryer and eventually supervising shifts.

Most of my free time goes into building my own research projects. I like taking a messy dataset, asking it a fairly simple question, and then seeing whether the answer survives some honest testing. A good amount of the time it doesn't, and I've learned to treat a clean null result as an actual result instead of something to quietly bury. Right now I'm leaning toward quantitative finance and investment research, where data and judgement get used together, though I'm still figuring out exactly where I'll land.

This site is for my DS4200 data visualization class, so it's small: this page, plus a page on Hekir, a project where I tested whether the sector labels used for S&P 500 companies actually match how those companies describe their own businesses. It has a real chart and everything.
